Add the beta to your iPhone Home Screen

  1. Open the Mayhem Genetics beta using Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Safari Share button, then choose Add to Home Screen (you may need to scroll).
  3. Choose Add. Open the Mayhem Genetics icon from the Home Screen to test it.

This is a website shortcut/web app, not a native TestFlight install. Some photo, barcode, and motion features may behave differently on iPhone.

What to test

Try Franklyn's research sources, open PubMed and YouTube links, check calculator navigation, and try manual food tracking. If your camera or barcode scan is unavailable, record what happened.
